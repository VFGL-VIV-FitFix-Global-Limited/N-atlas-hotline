# NYSC Registration and Service Year Guide

## What is NYSC?

The National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) is a one-year mandatory service programme for all Nigerian graduates under 30 years of age. After completing a degree or HND at a university or polytechnic, you must serve in NYSC before you can be formally employed in most organisations.

## Who Must Serve?

- All Nigerian graduates of universities and polytechnics (degree or HND holders)
- You must be under 30 years old at the time of graduation
- Both graduates of Nigerian institutions and Nigerians who studied abroad must serve

## Who is Exempt?

- Graduates who are 30 years or older at the time of graduation
- Members of the armed forces or police
- Graduates with certain disabilities (medical exemption)
- Those who have served in the military for at least 9 months

If you are exempt, you must obtain an NYSC exemption certificate.

## How to Register for NYSC

1. Your institution (university or polytechnic) submits your name to NYSC after graduation
2. Visit the NYSC portal at portal.nysc.org.ng
3. Register online by filling in your personal details, institution, course, and year of graduation
4. Upload your passport photograph and relevant documents
5. Print your call-up letter when mobilisation is announced
6. Report to the orientation camp in your state of deployment on the specified date

## What Happens During Service Year?

### Orientation Camp (3 weeks)
- Physical training and drills
- Skills acquisition workshops
- Community development education
- You receive your state posting and primary assignment

### Primary Assignment (11 months)
- You are posted to a Place of Primary Assignment (PPA) — a school, hospital, government office, company, or NGO
- You work at your PPA as a corps member
- Monthly allowance: approximately ₦33,000 (federal allowance) plus state allowance (varies by state)

### Community Development Service (CDS)
- You participate in community projects alongside your primary assignment
- CDS projects run throughout the service year

## Important Documents

After completing NYSC, you receive:
- **NYSC Discharge Certificate** — this is very important. Most employers require it.
- Keep it safe and make certified copies.

## How to Collect Your Discharge Certificate

1. Complete all service requirements (camp, primary assignment, CDS)
2. Your certificate is issued at the passing-out parade
3. If you miss it, visit the NYSC state secretariat to collect it
4. You can verify your certificate at portal.nysc.org.ng

## Tips

- Do not buy fake certificates or skip service — it is a criminal offence
- Keep your call-up number and all NYSC documents safe
- If you studied abroad, register early for mobilisation
- You can request redeployment for health, marital, or security reasons
