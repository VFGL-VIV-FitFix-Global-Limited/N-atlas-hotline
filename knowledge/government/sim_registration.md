# SIM Card Registration and NIN Linking in Nigeria

## What is SIM Registration?

The Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) requires that every SIM card in Nigeria be registered with the owner's details and linked to their NIN (National Identification Number). Unregistered or unlinked SIM cards may be deactivated.

## How to Register a New SIM Card

When you buy a new SIM card from any network (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile):

1. Visit a network-authorised registration centre or the network's service centre
2. Bring your NIN (the 11-digit number or NIN slip)
3. Bring a valid ID document
4. The agent will capture your biometrics and link the SIM to your NIN
5. Your SIM will be activated once registration is complete

## How to Link Your NIN to Your Existing SIM Card

Each network has a USSD code for NIN linking:

- **MTN:** Dial *785*NIN# (e.g., *785*12345678901#)
- **Airtel:** Dial *121*NIN# (e.g., *121*12345678901#)
- **Glo:** Dial *109*NIN# (e.g., *109*12345678901#)
- **9mobile:** Dial *200*NIN# (e.g., *200*12345678901#)

You can also link your NIN by:
- Visiting any network service centre with your NIN and valid ID
- Using the network's mobile app (if available)

## How Many SIMs Can Be Linked to One NIN?

Each NIN can be linked to a maximum of 4 SIM cards across all networks.

## What Happens if You Don't Link Your NIN?

- Your SIM card may be partially or fully barred (restricted from making calls or using data)
- You may lose access to your phone number
- Unlinked SIMs may eventually be deactivated

## How to Check if Your NIN is Linked

- Dial *346# from your phone to check your NIN status
- Visit your network's service centre
- Call your network's customer care line

## Tips

- Link your NIN as soon as possible to avoid service disruption
- If you have more than 4 SIM cards, you may need to deactivate some
- Only link SIMs at official network centres or authorised agents
- Never share your NIN with strangers or unofficial agents
- If you change your phone number, update your NIN records at a NIMC centre
