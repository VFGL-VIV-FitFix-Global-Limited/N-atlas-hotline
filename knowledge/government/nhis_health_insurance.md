# National Health Insurance in Nigeria

## What is NHIS?

The National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS), now known as the National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA), provides health insurance coverage to Nigerians. It helps you pay for medical care so you do not have to pay the full cost out of pocket when you are sick.

## Who is Covered?

- Federal government employees and their families are automatically enrolled
- State government employees in participating states
- Private sector workers (companies with 10+ employees should enrol their staff)
- Self-employed individuals can register voluntarily
- Informal sector workers can register through community-based schemes

## What Does NHIS Cover?

The basic plan covers:
- Outpatient care (doctor visits, prescriptions, basic tests)
- Inpatient care (hospital admission for up to 15 days per year)
- Maternity care (antenatal, delivery, postnatal for up to 4 pregnancies)
- Child care for children under 5
- Basic eye care
- Basic dental care
- Emergency care
- Some surgeries

What is NOT covered (basic plan):
- Cosmetic surgery
- Treatment for drug addiction
- HIV antiretroviral drugs (covered by separate government programmes)
- Some expensive surgeries and treatments
- Spectacles beyond basic

## How to Register (Formal Sector)

If you work for the government or a company that participates in NHIS:
1. Your employer registers you and your dependants (spouse + up to 4 children)
2. You are assigned a Health Maintenance Organisation (HMO)
3. Choose a primary healthcare provider (hospital or clinic) from the HMO's list
4. You receive an NHIS ID card
5. Visit your chosen healthcare provider when you need care — show your card

## How to Register (Informal Sector / Self-Employed)

1. Contact your state's health insurance agency or a participating HMO
2. Register yourself and your family
3. Pay the required premium (varies by state and plan)
4. Choose a healthcare provider
5. Receive your enrolment card

## Cost

- Formal sector: Your employer pays 10% of your basic salary, you contribute 5%
- Informal sector: Premiums vary by state, typically ₦5,000 to ₦30,000 per year per person
- Some states offer subsidised plans for vulnerable groups

## How to Use Your NHIS

1. Go to your registered primary healthcare provider (not just any hospital)
2. Show your NHIS ID card at registration
3. You may pay a small co-payment (typically 10% of the cost)
4. If you need specialist care, your primary provider will refer you

## Tips

- Always carry your NHIS card when visiting a hospital
- You can only use your designated healthcare provider unless it is an emergency
- If you are not satisfied with your healthcare provider, you can request a transfer after a minimum period
- Emergency care is covered at any NHIS-accredited facility
