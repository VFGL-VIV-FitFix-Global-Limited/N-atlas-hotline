# How to Renew an Expired Nigerian Passport

## When to Renew

Your Nigerian passport should be renewed when:
- It has expired or is about to expire (renew at least 6 months before expiry if you plan to travel)
- All the pages are full
- It is damaged or defaced
- Your name has changed (for example, after marriage)

## Documents You Need for Renewal

- Your old or expired passport (original)
- Valid NIN (National Identification Number)
- Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)
- Completed online application form from immigration.gov.ng
- Payment receipt for the renewal fee

If your name has changed:
- Marriage certificate or court order for name change
- Newspaper publication of the name change
- Sworn affidavit

## Step-by-Step Renewal Process

1. Go to immigration.gov.ng and log into your NIS account.
2. Select "Passport Renewal" and choose your booklet type (32-page or 64-page).
3. Fill in the form. Your details should match your NIN.
4. Upload your photo and documents.
5. Pay online and save your receipt.
6. Book a biometric appointment at your nearest passport office.
7. Attend on the appointment date with your old passport and original documents.
8. Collect your new passport when notified (usually by SMS or email).

## Processing Time

Renewal typically takes 3 to 6 weeks. Allow extra time during busy periods.

## Cost

- 32-page renewal: approximately ₦50,000
- 64-page renewal: approximately ₦100,000
- Confirm current fees at immigration.gov.ng before paying

## Tips

- Start the renewal process well before your passport expires
- You can still renew even if your passport expired years ago
- Keep a photocopy of your old passport pages for your records
- Never surrender your old passport to anyone other than an official NIS officer
