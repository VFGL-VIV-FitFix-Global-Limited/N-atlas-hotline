# Voter Registration and PVC Collection in Nigeria

## What is Voter Registration?

Voter registration is the process of registering with the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to be eligible to vote in Nigerian elections. After registration, you receive a Permanent Voter's Card (PVC), which you must present at your polling unit to vote.

## Who Can Register?

- Nigerian citizens aged 18 years and above
- You must not be currently registered elsewhere (double registration is an offence)
- You must be of sound mind
- You must not have been convicted of an election offence

## Documents You Need

- A valid means of identification: National ID card (NIN slip), driver's licence, or international passport
- Registration is FREE. Do not pay anyone.

## How to Register (Step by Step)

1. First, check if you are already registered by visiting the INEC Voter Verification Portal at cvr.inecnigeria.org
2. If you are not registered, visit your nearest INEC Local Government Area (LGA) office during the Continuous Voter Registration (CVR) exercise
3. Bring your valid ID
4. An INEC official will capture your details and biometrics (photo and fingerprints)
5. You will receive an acknowledgement
6. When your PVC is ready, INEC will announce collection dates
7. Collect your PVC from your registration centre — you need it to vote

## How to Transfer Your Voter Registration

If you have moved to a new area and want to vote closer to your new home:

1. Visit cvr.inecnigeria.org to check your current registration
2. Go to the INEC LGA office in your NEW area
3. Request a voter transfer
4. Bring your old PVC or registration details
5. Your registration will be moved to your new polling unit

## How to Replace a Damaged or Lost PVC

1. Visit your INEC LGA office
2. Report the damage or loss
3. Request a replacement
4. You may need to fill a form and provide identification

## How to Correct Mistakes on Your PVC

If your name, date of birth, or other details are wrong on your PVC:

1. Visit the INEC office where you registered
2. Bring supporting documents showing the correct information
3. Request a modification of your record

## Important Dates

INEC conducts Continuous Voter Registration (CVR) periodically. Registration usually closes several months before a general election. Check INEC's official announcements for current registration windows.

For the 2027 general elections, voter registration has been ongoing. The final cut-off date may be set in 2026 — check inecnigeria.org for updates.

## Tips

- Register early. Do not wait until close to election time.
- Arrive early at the INEC office — queues can be very long.
- Keep your PVC safe. Without it, you cannot vote.
- Do not allow anyone to collect your PVC on your behalf.
- Double registration is a criminal offence.
