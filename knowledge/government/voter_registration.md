# How to Register to Vote (INEC)

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) issues the Permanent Voter Card (PVC). You must be a Nigerian citizen, 18 or older, and register in the polling unit where you live.

## Registration

INEC opens Continuous Voter Registration (CVR) at announced periods. Take your NIN and a valid ID to an INEC office or designated centre. Your photo and fingerprints are captured. Collect your PVC when INEC announces collection.

Check your registration status on the INEC portal or at your local INEC office. Do not pay anyone for a PVC. INEC registration is free.
