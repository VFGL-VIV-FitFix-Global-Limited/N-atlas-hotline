# How to Register a Business with CAC in Nigeria

## What is CAC?

The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) is the agency responsible for registering businesses in Nigeria. Any business operating in Nigeria must be registered with CAC. Registration gives your business legal recognition, allows you to open a corporate bank account, sign contracts, access government programmes, and build credibility with customers.

## Types of Business Registration

### 1. Business Name Registration
- Best for small businesses, sole traders, and partnerships
- Cheaper and simpler to register
- The owner has unlimited personal liability
- Good for: shops, freelancers, small service providers, market traders

### 2. Limited Liability Company (Ltd/Gte)
- Best for growing businesses that want legal protection
- The company is a separate legal entity from the owner
- Owner's personal assets are protected (limited liability)
- Required for many government contracts and investor funding
- Good for: tech companies, larger businesses, companies seeking investment

## Requirements for Business Name Registration

- At least one proprietor who is a Nigerian citizen or resident
- Proposed business name (have 2-3 alternatives in case your first choice is taken)
- Business address in Nigeria
- Nature of business (what the business does)
- Valid NIN for all proprietors
- Passport photograph

## Requirements for Limited Company Registration

- At least 2 shareholders and 2 directors (can be the same people)
- Proposed company name
- Registered office address in Nigeria
- Minimum share capital of ₦100,000
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Valid NIN and passport photographs for all directors and shareholders
- Statement of compliance (Form CAC 1.1)

## Fees (2026 Estimates)

- Name reservation: ₦500 to ₦1,000
- Business Name registration: approximately ₦20,000
- Limited Company registration: ₦50,000 to ₦100,000 (varies by share capital)
- TIN (Tax Identification Number): issued free with registration

Note: These are approximate fees. Confirm current fees on the CAC portal before paying. If you use an agent or lawyer, their service charge is separate.

## How to Register (Step by Step)

1. Visit the CAC online portal at pre.cac.gov.ng
2. Create an account if you do not have one
3. Search for your proposed business name to check if it is available
4. Reserve the name (takes 24-48 hours for approval)
5. Once approved, complete the registration form online
6. Upload required documents
7. Pay the registration fee online
8. Submit your application
9. CAC reviews your application (2 to 7 business days)
10. If approved, download your registration certificate from the portal
11. Your TIN (Tax Identification Number) is generated automatically with registration

## After Registration

- Open a corporate bank account with your CAC certificate
- Register for tax with the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)
- Display your CAC registration number on your business premises
- File annual returns with CAC every year (failure attracts penalties)

## Tips

- Choose a unique business name. Common names are often rejected.
- Make sure the name does not contain restricted words like "Federal," "National," or "Nigeria" without special approval.
- Keep your CAC certificate safe — you will need it for many things.
- The entire process can now be done online. You do not need to visit a CAC office.
- Beware of agents who overcharge. You can register directly on the CAC portal.
