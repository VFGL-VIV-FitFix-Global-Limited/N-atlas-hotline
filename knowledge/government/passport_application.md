# How to Apply for a Nigerian International Passport

## What is the Nigerian International Passport?

The Nigerian international passport is your travel document issued by the Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS). It allows you to travel outside Nigeria. There are two types: the 32-page booklet and the 64-page booklet. The 64-page booklet is suitable for frequent travellers.

## Who Can Apply?

Any Nigerian citizen can apply for an international passport. You must have a valid NIN (National Identification Number) before you can apply.

## Documents Required (First-Time Applicants)

- Valid National Identification Number (NIN) — mandatory
- Birth certificate or age declaration
- Local government certificate of origin
- Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)
- Completed online application form
- Payment receipt

For applicants under 18 years:
- Consent letter signed by both parents or legal guardian
- Valid ID of both parents
- Child's NIN
- Child's birth certificate
- Both parents should be present at the biometric capture, or provide a court order authorising one parent

## Documents Required (Renewal)

- Your old or expired passport
- Valid NIN
- Recent passport photographs
- Completed online application form
- Payment receipt

## Current Fees

Passport fees have been updated. As of 2026:
- 32-page booklet (5-year validity): approximately ₦50,000
- 64-page booklet (10-year validity): approximately ₦100,000

Note: Fees may change. Always confirm the current fees on the official NIS website before paying. Do not pay to any unofficial person or account.

## How to Apply (Step by Step)

1. Visit the NIS portal at immigration.gov.ng and create an account or log in.
2. Click "Apply for Passport" and select your passport type (32-page or 64-page, new or renewal).
3. Fill in the application form. Make sure your name matches your NIN exactly.
4. Upload a passport photograph and any required documents.
5. Pay the fee online using your debit card or bank transfer. Save your payment receipt.
6. Note your application number, reference number, and validation number.
7. Use the validation number to book a biometric capture appointment at a passport office near you.
8. Attend the passport office on your appointment date with all your original documents.
9. Your fingerprints and photograph will be captured.
10. After processing, you will be notified when your passport is ready for collection.

## Processing Time

- Renewal: approximately 3 to 6 weeks
- Fresh application: approximately 6 to 8 weeks
- Processing may take longer during peak travel periods (December, summer holidays)

## Where to Apply

- NIS Headquarters, Abuja
- Any State Command Headquarters
- Any NIS Passport Office across the country
- Some Nigerian missions abroad (for diaspora applicants)

## Tips

- Apply early. Do not wait until you need to travel urgently.
- Make sure your NIN details match your passport application exactly. Mismatches cause rejection.
- Track your application status online at immigration.gov.ng
- Beware of passport scams. Only pay through the official NIS portal.
