# How to Apply for a Nigerian International Passport

The Nigerian international passport is issued by the Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS). You need it for international travel. There are two types: the standard passport (32 or 64 pages) and the official/diplomatic passport.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

1. Visit the NIS passport portal at portal.immigration.gov.ng.
2. Create an account and fill out the online application form.
3. Pay the passport fee online using Remita or at any designated bank.
4. Book an appointment for biometric capture at your preferred passport office.
5. Visit the passport office on your appointment date with all required documents.
6. Your biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) will be captured.
7. Collect your passport when notified — typically 2 to 6 weeks.

## Fees (as of 2026)

- 32-page passport (5 years): ₦35,000
- 64-page passport (10 years): ₦70,000

## Required Documents

- Completed online application form (printed)
- NIN slip or National ID card
- Birth certificate or age declaration
- Local government certificate of origin
- Two recent passport photographs (white background)
- Payment receipt

## Important Tips

- Apply online first — walk-in applications are no longer accepted.
- Do not use agents or touts — apply directly to avoid fraud.
- Track your application status on the NIS portal.
- For lost passports, file a police report first before applying for a replacement.
