# Bank Verification Number (BVN)

BVN is an 11-digit number that identifies you across Nigerian banks. You need it to open or operate most bank accounts.

## How to get a BVN

Visit any commercial bank branch (it does not have to be your own bank). Take a valid ID (NIN slip, voter card, driver's licence or passport). The bank captures your fingerprints and photo. Enrolment is free. Do not pay touts.

You can also dial *565*0# from the phone number linked to your account on some networks, or use your bank's USSD or app to retrieve a BVN you already have.

## If you forget your BVN

Ask your bank, use the bank USSD, or visit a branch with your ID and fingerprints. Report suspected BVN fraud to your bank and the CBN consumer complaints channels immediately.
