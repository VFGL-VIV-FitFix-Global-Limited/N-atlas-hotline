# Emergency and Helpline Numbers in Nigeria

## National Emergency Numbers

- **112** — National Emergency Number (police, fire, ambulance). Works on all networks. Free to call.
- **199** — Police Emergency
- **767** — Police Public Complaints Line

## Health Emergencies

- **NCDC Disease Reporting Hotline:** 0800-970-0010 (toll-free)
- **NCDC WhatsApp:** 08099999999
- **National Emergency Medical Service:** 112
- **Poison Information Centre:** 08033430039
- **Mental Health Helpline:** 08091116264 (Mental Health Foundation)

## Security

- **Police Emergency:** 199 or 112
- **DSS (State Security):** 09-6717034
- **NSCDC (Civil Defence):** 08032003766
- **FRSC (Road Accidents):** 122 (toll-free)
- **Fire Service:** 112

## Utility Emergencies

- **Power (Electricity):** Contact your local distribution company (Ikeja Electric, Eko Electric, etc.)
- **Gas Leak / Fire:** 112
- **DPR (Oil & Gas Emergency):** 08023289097

## Government Service Helplines

- **NIMC (NIN):** 07080606060
- **FIRS (Tax):** 0800-2255-3477
- **NCC (Telecoms Complaints):** 0800-2255-622
- **FRSC (Driving/Vehicle):** 122
- **NAFDAC (Food & Drugs):** 0800-1623-322
- **INEC (Voter Issues):** 09-2348846
- **JAMB:** 09062626364
- **NIS (Immigration/Passport):** Check immigration.gov.ng

## Anti-Corruption and Complaints

- **EFCC (Financial Crimes):** 08093aborticomplaint (check efcc.gov.ng)
- **ICPC (Corruption):** 08076369369
- **Consumer Protection Council:** 08056002266

## Women and Children

- **NAPTIP (Trafficking/Abuse):** 07030000203
- **Child Helpline:** 08001230000 (toll-free, 24/7, for children in distress)
- **Gender-Based Violence Helpline:** Check your state's Ministry of Women Affairs

## Tips

- 112 works from any phone, even without airtime
- When calling emergency numbers, clearly state: your location, the emergency, how many people are affected, and your phone number
- Save the most important numbers in your phone contacts
- For medical emergencies, go to the nearest hospital — do not wait for an ambulance in most cases

## Report a Problem

If any of these numbers are not working or you are not getting help:
- Visit the nearest police station in person
- Contact your Local Government Chairman's office
- Report on social media to the relevant agency
