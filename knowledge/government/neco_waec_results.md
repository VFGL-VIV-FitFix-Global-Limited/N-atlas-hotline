# How to Check WAEC and NECO Results

## Checking WAEC Results

The West African Examinations Council (WAEC) conducts the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE).

### How to Check Online

1. Visit the WAEC results portal at waecdirect.org
2. Enter your exam number (printed on your exam slip)
3. Select your exam year and type (May/June or November/December)
4. Enter the WAEC scratch card PIN and serial number
5. Click "Submit" to view your results

### How to Get a WAEC Scratch Card

- Buy from any WAEC office
- Buy from authorised banks and vendors
- Each scratch card can be used to check results a limited number of times (usually 5)
- Cost: approximately ₦1,000 (confirm current price)

## Checking NECO Results

The National Examinations Council (NECO) conducts the Senior Secondary Certificate Examination (SSCE).

### How to Check Online

1. Visit the NECO results portal at result.neco.gov.ng
2. Enter your exam number
3. Enter the token (PIN) from a NECO scratch card
4. Select your exam year
5. Click "Check Result" to view your grades

### How to Get a NECO Token/Scratch Card

- Buy from NECO offices
- Buy from authorised vendors
- Cost: approximately ₦500 to ₦1,000

## Understanding Your Grades

- A1 (Excellent) to C6 (Credit) — these are credit passes
- D7 and E8 — these are passes but not credit level
- F9 — Fail

For JAMB admission, you typically need at least 5 credits including English and Mathematics.

## Getting a Replacement Certificate

If you have lost your WAEC or NECO certificate:

### WAEC
1. Visit waecnigeria.org
2. Apply for a "Confirmation of Result" or certificate attestation
3. Pay the required fee
4. Submit your application with a passport photo and details of your exam

### NECO
1. Visit any NECO state office
2. Apply for a replacement or verification
3. Provide your exam number, year, and personal details
4. Pay the processing fee

## Tips

- Keep your original certificates and result slips in a safe place
- Make multiple photocopies and store them separately
- Your exam number is important — write it down and keep it safe
- If your results are withheld, contact the exam body to find out why
