# How to Get Your BVN (Bank Verification Number)

## What is BVN?

The Bank Verification Number (BVN) is an 11-digit number that identifies you across all banks in Nigeria. It was introduced by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) to reduce fraud and protect your bank accounts. Your BVN links all your bank accounts together using your biometric data (fingerprints and photo).

Every Nigerian with a bank account must have a BVN. Many services now require BVN, including: opening new bank accounts, financial transactions, mobile money services, and some government programmes.

## Who Needs a BVN?

- Every Nigerian with a bank account or who wants to open one
- You only need ONE BVN — it works across all banks
- Nigerians abroad can also register for BVN at designated centres

## How to Register for BVN

1. Visit any commercial bank branch in Nigeria where you have an account (or any bank if you are opening a new account)
2. Tell the customer service officer you want to register for BVN
3. Provide your valid ID (NIN slip, driver's licence, passport, or voter's card)
4. Your biometrics will be captured: fingerprints, facial photograph, and signature
5. Provide your phone number and other personal details
6. Your BVN will be generated and sent to your phone via SMS, usually within 24 hours

## Cost

BVN registration is FREE at your bank. Do not pay anyone for BVN registration.

## How to Check Your BVN

If you have forgotten your BVN, you can retrieve it:

- Dial *565*0# from the phone number linked to your bank account
- A small charge (about ₦20) applies for this USSD service
- Your BVN will be displayed on your phone screen
- You can also visit your bank branch and ask them to look up your BVN

## How to Link Your BVN to a New Bank Account

When you open a new bank account, provide your BVN. The bank will verify your biometrics against the BVN database. Your new account will automatically be linked to your BVN.

## How to Update Your BVN Details

If your phone number, name, or other details have changed:

1. Visit your bank branch
2. Request a BVN data update
3. Provide supporting documents (for example, marriage certificate for a name change)
4. The bank will submit the update to the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS)

## Important Notes

- Your BVN is confidential. Never share it with strangers or on social media.
- Scammers may call you pretending to be your bank and ask for your BVN. Your bank will never ask for your BVN over the phone.
- One person should have only one BVN. Multiple BVNs are flagged by the system.
- BVN does not expire.
