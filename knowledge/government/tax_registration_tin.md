# Tax Registration and TIN in Nigeria

## What is a TIN?

A Tax Identification Number (TIN) is a unique number assigned to every taxpayer in Nigeria. It is issued by the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) for federal taxes or by your State Internal Revenue Service (SIRS) for state taxes. Every working Nigerian and every registered business must have a TIN.

## Who Needs a TIN?

- Every Nigerian who earns income (employed or self-employed)
- Every registered business or company
- Required for: opening corporate bank accounts, government contracts, filing tax returns, property transactions, and importing goods

## How to Get a TIN (Individuals)

1. Visit the FIRS JTB (Joint Tax Board) portal at jtb.gov.ng
2. Click on "TIN Registration"
3. Fill in your personal details: name, NIN, address, occupation, employer details
4. Submit the form
5. Your TIN will be generated and sent to your email
6. You can also visit any FIRS office with your NIN and a valid ID to register in person

## How to Get a TIN (Businesses)

If you register your business with CAC, a TIN is now automatically generated during the registration process. If you registered before this was implemented or need to check:

1. Visit jtb.gov.ng
2. Search for your company's TIN using your CAC registration number
3. If not found, register using your CAC certificate and company details

## Cost

TIN registration is FREE. Do not pay anyone for a TIN.

## How to Check Your TIN

- Visit jtb.gov.ng and use the TIN verification tool
- Call the FIRS helpline

## Types of Taxes in Nigeria

- **Personal Income Tax (PIT):** Tax on your salary or income (paid by individuals)
- **Company Income Tax (CIT):** Tax on business profits (30% for large companies, 20% for medium, 0% for small companies with less than ₦25 million turnover)
- **Value Added Tax (VAT):** 7.5% on goods and services
- **Withholding Tax (WHT):** Deducted at source on certain payments
- **Capital Gains Tax:** Tax on profit from selling assets

## Tips

- Keep your TIN safe — you will need it for many official transactions
- File your tax returns annually, even if no tax is due
- Small businesses with turnover below ₦25 million may be exempt from Company Income Tax
- Employees: your employer usually deducts your income tax (PAYE) before paying your salary
