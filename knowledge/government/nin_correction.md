# How to Correct or Update Your NIN Details

## When You Need to Update

You may need to update your NIN record if:

- Your name was misspelled during registration
- You changed your name (for example, after marriage)
- Your date of birth was entered wrongly
- You need to update your phone number or address
- Your photo needs updating

## How to Update Your NIN Details

1. Visit any NIMC enrolment centre near you.
2. Tell the officer you need to modify your NIN record.
3. Bring your original NIN slip and the supporting document for the change (for example, a marriage certificate for a name change, or a birth certificate for date of birth correction).
4. The officer will process the modification and capture new biometrics if needed.
5. A modification fee may apply. Ask the officer for the current fee.

## How to Replace a Lost NIN Slip

If you have lost your NIN slip but remember your NIN, you can:

- Dial *346# to retrieve your NIN
- Visit any NIMC centre to request a reprint

If you have forgotten your NIN entirely, visit a NIMC centre with a valid ID and your biometrics will be used to find your record.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Do not register for NIN more than once. Multiple registrations can cause problems.
- Make sure your name matches exactly across all your documents (NIN, BVN, passport, voter card). Mismatches cause delays.
- Do not pay touts or middlemen. NIN services are available directly at NIMC centres.
