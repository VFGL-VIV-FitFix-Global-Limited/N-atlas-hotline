# How to Register for NIN (National Identification Number)

The National Identification Number (NIN) is an 11-digit number issued by the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC). Every Nigerian citizen and legal resident is required to have a NIN. It is needed for SIM card registration, bank accounts, passport applications, JAMB registration and many government services.

## Who Needs a NIN?

Every Nigerian citizen aged 0 and above, as well as legal residents of Nigeria, must register for a NIN. Parents can register newborns and children under 16.

## How to Register

1. Visit any NIMC enrollment centre near you. You can find centres at local government offices, banks, and mobile registration points.
2. Bring your original documents — no photocopies needed for the initial visit.
3. Fill out the registration form at the centre.
4. Your biometrics will be captured: ten fingerprints, facial photograph, and iris scan.
5. You will receive a transaction slip with a tracking ID.
6. Your NIN will be sent to you via SMS within 24-48 hours.

## Required Documents (bring at least one)

- Birth certificate or declaration of age
- Valid passport
- Previous national ID card
- Driver's licence
- Voter's card (PVC)
- A letter from a recognised community leader (if no formal documents)

## Fees

NIN registration is FREE. Do not pay anyone to register you for NIN. Report anyone asking for payment to NIMC.

## What If I Forgot My NIN?

Dial *346# on your registered phone number to retrieve your NIN. You can also visit any NIMC office with your fingerprints for retrieval.
