# How to Register for NIN (National Identification Number)

## What is NIN?

The National Identification Number (NIN) is an 11-digit number assigned to every Nigerian citizen and legal resident. It is issued by the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC). The NIN is now required for SIM card registration, bank accounts, JAMB registration, passport applications, voter registration, and many other government services. Every Nigerian needs a NIN.

## Who Needs a NIN?

Every Nigerian citizen from birth needs a NIN. Parents or guardians can register children from birth to age 15. If you are 16 years or older, you must register in person. Foreign residents with valid immigration documents can also register.

## Documents You Need

Bring at least ONE original valid identity document:

- Old national ID card
- Driver's licence
- Voter's card (temporary or permanent)
- Nigerian international passport
- Birth certificate or declaration of age
- Certificate of origin
- Letter of introduction from your local government
- School ID card (recognised institutions)
- NHIS card
- Tax clearance certificate

For children: bring the child's birth certificate and the parent or guardian's own valid ID.

## How to Register (Step by Step)

1. Check if you already have a NIN by dialing *346# on your phone.
2. If you do not have one, visit the NIMC pre-enrolment portal at penrol.nimc.gov.ng to fill your details online, OR go directly to an enrolment centre.
3. If you pre-enrol online, print your pre-enrolment summary sheet (it has a barcode).
4. Visit your nearest NIMC enrolment centre or approved registration point with your original documents.
5. The officer will verify your documents and capture your biometrics (photo, fingerprints, signature).
6. Check all your details on the screen before the officer submits.
7. You will receive an enrolment slip. Keep it safe.
8. Your NIN will be sent to your phone via SMS, usually within 24 to 72 hours.

## Cost

First-time NIN registration is FREE. You do not need to pay anybody. If someone asks you to pay for NIN registration, it is likely a scam.

Modification of records (name change, etc.) may attract a small fee. Card replacement also has a fee. Check with NIMC directly.

## Where to Register

- NIMC enrolment centres in every state. Find your nearest one at nimccbs.gov.ng
- Ward-level registration points (being expanded nationwide)
- Some approved banks and telco offices also offer NIN enrolment

## How to Check Your NIN

- Dial *346# from any phone
- Visit nimc.gov.ng
- Check your NIN slip

## How to Link Your NIN to Your Phone

Dial *346# and follow the instructions to link your NIN to your SIM card. This is required by law. Each NIN can be linked to a maximum of 4 SIM cards.
