# How to Get a Birth Certificate in Nigeria

## What is a Birth Certificate?

A birth certificate is an official document that records the birth of a child. In Nigeria, birth registration is handled by the National Population Commission (NPC). Every child born in Nigeria has the right to a birth certificate, and it is required for school enrolment, NIN registration, passport application, and many other official purposes.

## Who Needs a Birth Certificate?

- Every child born in Nigeria should be registered within 60 days of birth
- Late registration is allowed for older children and adults who were never registered
- Both Nigerian citizens and children born to foreign parents in Nigeria can be registered

## Cost

Birth registration is FREE if done within 60 days of birth. Late registration may attract a small fee (varies by state).

## How to Register a Birth (Within 60 Days)

1. Visit the nearest NPC registration centre. These are found in:
   - Local Government Secretariats
   - Primary Health Care centres
   - Hospitals and maternity centres
   - NPC state and local government offices
2. Bring the child and the following documents:
   - Hospital birth notification or delivery record (if born in a hospital)
   - Parent's valid ID (NIN, voter's card, passport, or driver's licence)
   - Parent's marriage certificate (if available)
3. Fill in the birth registration form (Form B)
4. The registrar will process the registration
5. Collect the birth certificate — it is usually issued immediately or within a few days

## How to Register a Late Birth (After 60 Days)

If the child is older than 60 days and was never registered:

1. Visit the NPC office at your Local Government Area
2. Bring:
   - Parent's valid ID
   - Sworn affidavit of birth (obtained from a court or magistrate)
   - Two passport photographs of the child
   - Hospital records (if available)
3. A small processing fee may apply for late registration
4. The NPC officer will verify the information and process the registration
5. You will receive the birth certificate

## Online Registration

The NPC has launched an online birth registration portal. You can start the process at birthregistration.gov.ng:

1. Visit the portal and create an account
2. Fill in the child's details and upload required documents
3. Select a collection centre near you
4. Complete verification at the centre
5. Collect the certificate

## What Information is on the Birth Certificate?

- Child's full name
- Date of birth
- Place of birth
- Sex
- Father's name and nationality
- Mother's name and nationality
- Registration number

## Why is a Birth Certificate Important?

- Required for NIN registration
- Required for school admission
- Required for passport application
- Required for JAMB registration
- Needed for age verification
- Needed for inheritance and legal matters
- Required for some employment

## Tips

- Register your child's birth as soon as possible — it is free within 60 days
- Make sure the name and details are spelled correctly
- Keep the original certificate in a safe place and make photocopies
- If you were never registered, you can do late registration at any age
