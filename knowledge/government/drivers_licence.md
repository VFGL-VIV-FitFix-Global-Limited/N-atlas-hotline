# How to Get or Renew a Driver's Licence in Nigeria

## What is a Driver's Licence?

A driver's licence is an official document issued by the Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC) that permits you to drive on Nigerian roads. It is a legal requirement — driving without a valid licence is an offence.

## Types of Driver's Licence

- Class A: Motorcycles
- Class B: Cars, SUVs, and light vehicles (up to 3,500 kg)
- Class C: Trucks and heavy vehicles (3,500 kg to 16,000 kg)
- Class D: Articulated vehicles (trailers, tankers)
- Class E: Vehicles carrying passengers for hire (commercial)
- Class F: Agricultural machinery and tractors
- Class J: Construction vehicles

Most people need Class B for private cars.

## Requirements for New Applicants

- You must be at least 18 years old
- Valid NIN (National Identification Number)
- Certificate from an approved driving school
- Medical fitness report (especially for commercial and heavy vehicle classes)
- Passport-sized photographs
- Valid means of identification

## How to Apply for a New Driver's Licence

1. Attend an approved driving school and obtain your certificate
2. Visit the FRSC portal at nigeriadriverslicence.frsc.gov.ng
3. Select "Apply New" and fill in the application form
4. Upload your driving school certificate and passport photo
5. Pay the application fee online
6. Book an appointment at a Driver's Licence Centre (DLC) near you
7. Visit the DLC for biometric capture (photo and fingerprints)
8. You will receive a temporary licence valid for 60 days
9. Your permanent licence will be produced and sent to the DLC for collection
10. You will receive an SMS when it is ready

## How to Renew an Expired Licence

1. Go to nigeriadriverslicence.frsc.gov.ng
2. Select the renewal option
3. Enter your date of birth and your expired licence number
4. Fill in the renewal form
5. Pay the renewal fee online
6. Print your payment receipt
7. Visit a DLC or VIO office with your receipt and old licence
8. Complete biometric capture
9. Receive a 60-day temporary licence
10. Collect your new permanent licence when notified by SMS

## Fees (2026 Estimates)

- New licence (3-year validity): approximately ₦20,000 per year
- New licence (5-year validity): approximately ₦35,000 per year
- Renewal fees are similar to new licence fees

Note: Fees may have changed. Always check the official FRSC portal for current fees.

## Validity

- Standard driver's licence: 3 years or 5 years (you choose during application)
- You must renew before it expires to avoid penalties
- Driving with an expired licence attracts a fine

## Tips

- Apply for renewal at least one month before your licence expires
- Make sure your name and details match your NIN
- Keep your temporary licence with you while waiting for the permanent one
- Only learn to drive at an FRSC-approved driving school
- The online portal is the fastest way to apply — avoid unnecessary middlemen
