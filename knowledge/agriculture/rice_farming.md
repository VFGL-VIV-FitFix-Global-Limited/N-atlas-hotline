# Rice Farming Guide

Nigeria grows rice in lowland (fadama) and upland areas. Good seed, water control and timely weeding raise yields.

## Planting

Upland rice is planted with the rains. Lowland rice needs a reliable water source. Use certified seed such as FARO varieties recommended by the National Cereals Research Institute (NCRI).

## Land and Fertiliser

Clear and level the field. For lowland rice, puddle the soil before transplanting. Apply NPK at planting and urea as a top dressing as advised by your ADP extension officer. Do not guess rates — soil and variety matter.

## Weeds, Pests and Birds

Weed early, especially in the first 40 days. Watch for stem borers and rice blast (brown spots on leaves). Scare birds at grain-filling stage. Ask your ADP about approved pesticides and safe use.

## Harvest

Harvest when about 80–85% of grains are golden. Dry paddy to a safe moisture level before storage to prevent mould.

For loans and inputs, ask about NIRSAL, Bank of Agriculture, and the CBN Anchor Borrowers Programme at your ADP or commercial bank.
