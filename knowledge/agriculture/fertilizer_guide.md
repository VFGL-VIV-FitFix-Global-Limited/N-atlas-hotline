# Fertilizer Types and Application

Common blends in Nigeria include NPK 15-15-15 and urea. Rates depend on crop, soil and variety. Ask your ADP extension officer before you buy.

## Practical rules

- Apply NPK at or soon after planting for maize, rice and vegetables.
- Top-dress urea when the crop is growing strongly, not on dry soil if rain is not coming.
- Do not place fertilizer against the stem. Keep a small gap.
- Store bags off the ground, away from rain.

Counterfeit fertilizer is common. Buy from known agro-dealers or government e-wallet / input programmes. For loans and subsidised inputs, ask about NIRSAL, Bank of Agriculture and CBN Anchor Borrowers at your ADP.
