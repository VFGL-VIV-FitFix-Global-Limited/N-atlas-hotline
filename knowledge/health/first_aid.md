# First Aid Basics

This is not a diagnosis. For life-threatening emergencies call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

## Bleeding

Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth. Keep pressure. Raise the limb if you can. Do not remove a deeply stuck object — pad around it.

## Burns

Cool with clean running water for at least 10 minutes. Do not put toothpaste, oil or raw egg on a burn. Cover loosely with a clean cloth.

## Choking (adult)

If the person cannot speak or cough, stand behind them and give upward abdominal thrusts. Call for help.

## Unconscious but breathing

Place them on their side (recovery position). Keep the airway clear. Get medical help.
