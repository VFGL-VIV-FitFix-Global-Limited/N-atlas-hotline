# COVID-19 in Nigeria

## What is COVID-19?

COVID-19 is a respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. While the pandemic phase has passed, COVID-19 still circulates and can cause illness, especially in vulnerable populations.

## Symptoms

- Fever
- Cough (usually dry)
- Tiredness and body aches
- Sore throat
- Loss of taste or smell
- Headache
- Difficulty breathing (severe cases)
- Diarrhoea (sometimes)

Some people have mild or no symptoms at all.

## Prevention

- Wash hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds
- Use hand sanitiser (at least 60% alcohol) when soap is not available
- Cover your mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing (use elbow or tissue)
- Stay home if you feel unwell
- Get vaccinated if eligible — COVID-19 vaccines are available at government health facilities
- In crowded spaces or during outbreaks, consider wearing a face mask

## Vaccination

- COVID-19 vaccines are safe and effective
- Available free at government health centres and designated vaccination sites
- Multiple vaccine types available in Nigeria
- Check with your state primary health care board for current availability
- Booster doses may be recommended for certain groups

## What to Do if You Have Symptoms

1. Stay home and isolate from family members if possible
2. Wear a face mask around others
3. Rest and drink plenty of fluids
4. Take paracetamol for fever and body aches
5. Call the NCDC hotline for guidance: 0800-970-0010
6. Go to the hospital if you have difficulty breathing, chest pain, or confusion

## When to Go to the Hospital

- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Persistent chest pain or pressure
- Confusion or inability to stay awake
- Bluish lips or face
- Symptoms worsening after initial improvement

## High-Risk Groups

People at higher risk of severe COVID-19:
- Elderly (60 years and above)
- People with chronic conditions: diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, kidney disease, cancer
- People with weakened immune systems (including those with HIV)
- Pregnant women

These groups should prioritise vaccination and seek medical attention early.

## Tips

- COVID-19 symptoms overlap with malaria, typhoid, and flu — get tested to know what you have
- Misinformation about COVID-19 is common — get information from NCDC and WHO
- Vaccines do not cause infertility or contain tracking chips — these are myths
- Regular handwashing and good hygiene prevent many diseases, not just COVID-19
